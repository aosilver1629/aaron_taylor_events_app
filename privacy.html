Privacy Policy

SF Activities · Last updated August 29, 2026

Who we are

SF Activities is a personal, non-commercial project operated by an individual in San Francisco, California. It suggests local events by text message to a small number of recipients who have given prior consent. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

Information collected

  • Mobile phone number — submitted by the recipient through the opt-in form at https://aosilver1629.github.io/aaron_taylor_events_app/signup.html.
  • Message replies — the responses recipients send indicating interest in a suggested event, and any opt-out or help requests.
  • Message delivery records — timestamps and delivery status returned by the messaging provider.

No names, addresses, payment information, location data or browsing data are collected. No tracking and no cookies are used. The web opt-in form linked above is the only way phone numbers are collected for this service.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate the service: to send suggested events to consenting recipients, to record their responses so that mutually agreed events can be added to a shared private calendar, and to honor opt-out requests.

Information is never used for advertising, marketing or profiling.

Information sharing

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are never shared with, sold to, rented to, or disclosed to any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes.

No information collected through this service — including opt-in data and consent records — is shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes under any circumstances.

The only third party involved is the licensed messaging provider used to transmit the messages, which processes phone numbers and message content solely to deliver them on our behalf. Information may also be disclosed if required by law.

How information is stored

Phone numbers and replies are stored in a private database accessible only to the operator. They are retained only for as long as the recipient continues to use the service.

Opting out and deleting your information

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages immediately. To have your phone number and message history deleted entirely, email the address below and the request will be honored within 30 days.

Children

This service is not directed to anyone under 18, and information is not knowingly collected from children.
