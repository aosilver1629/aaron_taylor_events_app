SF Activities · Last updated August 28, 2026

About this service

SF Activities is a personal, non-commercial project operated by an individual. It sends text messages containing suggested local events in the San Francisco Bay Area — concerts, festivals, markets and similar activities — to a small number of recipients who have each given prior consent. Recipients reply to indicate whether they are interested in an event.

This service is not a business, does not sell anything, and sends no marketing, promotional or advertising messages of any kind.

Consent

Messages are sent only to individuals who have given express prior consent to receive them. Consent is given verbally or in writing directly to the operator. There is no public sign-up form, and numbers are never added to this service by any other means.

Consent to receive messages is not a condition of any purchase, because nothing is sold through this service.

Message frequency

You can expect up to 5 messages per week. Frequency varies depending on how many events are found.

Cost

There is no charge from us for this service. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan and carrier.

How to stop messages

Reply STOP to any message to be unsubscribed immediately. You will receive one confirmation message, and no further messages will be sent unless you opt back in.

To opt back in after unsubscribing, reply START.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message for support information, or email the operator at the address below.
