SF Activities

Text alerts for local San Francisco events

What this is

SF Activities is a personal, non-commercial project operated by an individual in San Francisco, California. It suggests local events by text message — concerts, festivals, markets and similar activities — to a small number of recipients who have each given prior consent. Recipients reply to indicate whether they are interested in an event.

Nothing is sold through this service, and no marketing or promotional messages are ever sent. Messages are limited to suggested local events for recipients who have opted in.
